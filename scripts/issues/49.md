**What you'll build:** A one-page, phone-friendly board of LedgerLoop's UPI payments with a status badge on each and the day's totals, live on the internet as a Codex Site. You describe it, Codex builds it, checks it in the in-app browser and deploys it. About 20 to 30 minutes.

**You need:** the Codex app, signed in, with Sites. On a Free or Go plan, redeem the sponsored month Codexing gives you first. Nothing to clone: this ticket doesn't use the LedgerLoop code.

**Easiest way in:** in Codex, say `@Codexing start`. It checks your computer is ready and hands you this ticket.

### Do it yourself

In Codex, open a new empty folder, start a chat, switch on plan mode and paste this:

```text
@Sites Build a one-page payments board and deploy it as a Site. Download the data from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id8nxt-hub/ledgerloop/main/sites/payments.json (13 UPI payments; money is in paise, 100 paise = ₹1). Show each payment as a row: payment id, invoice, payer, amount in rupees (₹1,234.50) and a coloured badge with its status exactly as the data says: Settled, Pending, Failed or Refunded. At the top show three totals: settled, refunded and net (settled minus refunded). It must work on a phone. Also serve /api/payments (each payment with id, invoice, amount_paise and status) and /api/summary (settled_paise, refunded_paise, net_paise, and counts with settled, pending, failed and refunded). Open it in the in-app browser and check it before you deploy.
```

Approve the plan, let Codex build and deploy, and open the link it gives you.

### Done when

The board shows a net of ₹7,929.46, and each payment's badge matches its status in the data.

### Get it verified

Share the Site with anyone with the link. Then say **Codexing, check my work** and paste the link. Codexing reads your live Site and a hidden check decides; only the Site's link is sent.

### Stretch (advanced)

Filter by status and search by invoice, a proper empty state, one reusable status badge used everywhere, and a dark mode that follows the system.
