**What you'll build:** A small web app that lists LedgerLoop's invoices from a database and gets every GST total right to the paisa, live on the internet as a Codex Site. You describe it, Codex builds and deploys it. About 20 to 30 minutes.

**You need:** the Codex app, signed in, with Sites. On a Free or Go plan, redeem the sponsored month Codexing gives you first. Nothing to clone: this ticket doesn't use the LedgerLoop code.

**Easiest way in:** in Codex, say `@Codexing start`. It checks your computer is ready and hands you this ticket.

### Do it yourself

In Codex, open a new empty folder, start a chat, switch on plan mode and paste this:

```text
@Sites Build a small invoice ledger and deploy it as a Site. Download the data from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id8nxt-hub/ledgerloop/main/sites/invoices.json (12 invoices with their lines; money is in paise, 100 paise = ₹1) and load it into a Sites database. Show one page listing every invoice: number, customer and total in rupees. Add two JSON routes: /api/invoices (every invoice with number, customer, taxable_paise, gst_paise and total_paise) and /api/invoices/<number> (one invoice, same fields). GST rule: for each GST rate on an invoice, add up the taxable value of its lines at that rate, apply the rate once and round half up to the paisa. Never round line by line. Show me the plan before you build.
```

Approve the plan, let Codex build and deploy, and open the link it gives you.

### Done when

`/api/invoices/INV-2026-0187` on your Site shows `"total_paise": 33310` (₹333.10).

### Get it verified

Share the Site with anyone with the link. Then say **Codexing, check my work** and paste the link. Codexing reads your live Site and a hidden check decides; only the Site's link is sent.

### Stretch (advanced)

Compute every total in SQL from the stored lines, add /api/gst-summary (taxable value and GST per rate across all invoices), and a form that adds a line to an invoice and shows the new total.
