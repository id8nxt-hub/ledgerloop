Our client's CA flagged **INV-2026-0187** (Nimbus Analytics LLP): we billed **₹333.09**, their
books say **₹333.10**.

The invoice mixes GST rates:

| Item | Qty | Unit price | GST |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding kit (printed) | 1 | ₹149.00 | 5% |
| SMS reminder pack (100) | 2 | ₹49.90 | 18% |
| SMS top-up (100) | 1 | ₹49.90 | 18% |

Single-rate invoices look right. Mixed-rate ones are sometimes a paisa out.
