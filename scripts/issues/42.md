---
title: "Settled UPI payments show Pending"
labels: [stack:frontend, level:all, domain:payments, tier:1]
base_tag: t1-base
---
On the payments screen, UPI payments that the ledger has already settled are shown as **Pending**.
The settlement page counts them as settled, so the two screens disagree. Failed and refunded
payments look right.

To see it: `pnpm dev`, then http://localhost:5173/#/payments. For a fresh one, pay
INV-2026-0187 at http://localhost:5174/?invoice=INV-2026-0187 with the mock UPI app: the checkout
says settled, the payments screen says Pending.
